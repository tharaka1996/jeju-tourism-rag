Title: Guide to Jeju's Prime Beaches and Coastal Landmarks
Source: Jeju Tourism Organization (VisitJeju)
Access Date: 2026-10-15
Category: Coastal Attractions & Marine Recreation

Major Beaches of Jeju Island:
Jeju's coastline features pristine waters ranging from cobalt blue to emerald turquoise, shaped by white pulverized seashell sand, black volcanic sand, and dramatic basalt formations.

1. Hyeopjae Beach & Geumneung Beach (Western Coast, Hallim-eup):
   - Signature Features: Renowned for transparent cobalt water, shallow slope extending hundreds of meters offshore, and picture-perfect views of Biyangdo Island floating on the horizon.
   - Suitability: Highly recommended for families with young children due to low tidal currents and vast tidal flat pools.
   - Nearby Amenities: Hallim Park, palm tree groves, camping grounds, and seaside cafes along the coastal road.

2. Hamdeok Beach (Northeastern Coast, Jocheon-eup):
   - Signature Features: Famous for vibrant emerald water divided into three distinct bays by natural basalt breakwaters and arched wooden bridges.
   - Landmarks: Seoubong Peak borders the eastern end, offering an easy 15-minute coastal hike with breathtaking panoramic vistas over the entire bay and seasonal canola or cosmos blossoms.
   - Night Life: Vibrant beachfront promenade featuring convenience stores, seafood restaurants, and street busking performances.

3. Jungmun Saekdal Beach (Southern Coast, Seogwipo Jungmun Resort):
   - Signature Features: Distinctive four-colored sands (black, white, red, and gray) nestled beneath high volcanic coastal cliffs.
   - Water Conditions: Strong oceanic swell and tall rolling waves make Jungmun the premier destination for surfing, bodyboarding, and parasailing in South Korea.
   - Safety Note: Powerful undertow currents and rip tides occur frequently; swimmers must adhere strictly to lifeguard zone flags.

4. Woljeongri Beach & Gwangchigi Beach (Eastern Coast):
   - Woljeongri: Beloved for its wind turbines spinning over shallow turquoise waters and trendy beachside cafes featuring outdoor photo chairs.
   - Gwangchigi Beach: Located adjacent to Seongsan Ilchulbong. During low tide, wide volcanic moss-covered green reefs emerge, providing the most iconic photographic backdrop of Sunrise Peak. Swimming is prohibited at Gwangchigi due to sharp volcanic rocks.

Seasonal Operating Hours and Swimming Regulations:
- Official Bathing Season: July 1 through August 31 annually (some beaches offer extended night swimming until 21:00).
- Lifeguard Operations: Stationed from 10:00 to 19:00 during the official bathing season. Outside the official season, swimming is unsupervised and at personal risk.
- Marine Ecology Notice: Jellyfish warning flags are deployed during warm August waters; beachgoers should refrain from touching stranded marine organisms.
