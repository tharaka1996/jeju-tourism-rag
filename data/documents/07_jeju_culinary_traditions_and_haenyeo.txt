Title: Jeju Culinary Heritage, Local Specialties, and Haenyeo Diver Culture
Source: Jeju Special Self-Governing Province Cultural Heritage Bureau
Access Date: 2026-10-15
Category: Cultural Heritage, Gastronomy & Living Traditions

The Haenyeo Culture of Jeju:
Designated by UNESCO in 2016 as an Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, the Haenyeo (female divers of Jeju) represent a semi-matriarchal society of ocean harvest practitioners.
- Traditional Techniques: Diving without oxygen tanks (breath-hold free diving) to ocean depths of 10 to 20 meters for up to two minutes per dive.
- Diving Equipment: Goggles (mul-angyeong), lead weight belts, round floats with hanging net bags (tewak and mangsari), and harvesting irons (bitchang).
- Bulteok and Community Organization: Divers gather at stone windbreaks called Bulteok around communal campfires before and after harvesting to warm themselves, check equipment, teach younger divers, and establish sustainable harvest quotas. Harvesting seasons strictly forbid taking undersized shells (e.g., abalone under 7 cm or conch under 7 cm) to maintain ocean replenishment.
- Haenyeo Museum: Situated in Hado-ri, Gujwa-eup, exhibiting the lifeways, tools, songs, and historical anti-Japanese resistance led by Haenyeo.

Iconic Culinary Specialties of Jeju Island:
1. Jeju Heukdwaeji (Jeju Black Pork):
   - Features: Distinctive breed of domestic pig raised in Jeju's volcanic terrain with superior intramuscular marbling, yielding tender meat and sweet fat flavor.
   - Traditional Serving: Grilled over charcoal and dipped into Meljot (a rich, savory salted anchovy dip simmered on the grill grating with garlic and hot peppers).
2. Okdom-gui (Grilled Red Tilefish):
   - Features: Prized white fish indigenous to deep southern waters around Jeju. Lightly salted, sun-dried in ocean breezes, and pan-fried with delicate sesame oil.
3. Momguk (Sargassum Seaweed Stew):
   - Features: A celebratory local soup boiled from rich pork bone broth infused with Gulfweed (Mom), seasonal radishes, and buckwheat flour to create a velvety, warming texture.
4. Omegi-tteok (Millet Rice Cakes):
   - Features: Traditional round cakes made from glutinous black millet dough coated in whole boiled red beans and stuffed with sweet red bean paste.
5. Hallabong Citrus:
   - Features: A premium hybrid tangerine characterized by a raised stem knob resembling the peak of Mount Hallasan. Harvested in winter, it is renowned for its high brix sweetness and fragrant peel.
