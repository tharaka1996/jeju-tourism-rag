Title: Jeju Traveler Safety, Weather, Emergency Services, and Tourist Facilities
Source: Jeju Tourist Information Center & Korea Tourism Organization
Access Date: 2026-10-15
Category: Travel Safety, Climate & Tourist Infrastructure

Tourist Information Centers and Support Services:
- Jeju Tourist Information Center (JTIC):
  - Phone: 064-740-6000 (Open 09:00 to 18:00 daily).
  - Languages Supported: Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese.
  - Locations: Jeju International Airport Arrival Hall (Gate 2), Jeju Port Passenger Terminal, and Seogwipo City Hall annex.
- Korea Travel Helpline (1330):
  - 24/7 nationwide telephone helpline providing emergency interpretation, tourist complaint resolution, bus schedules, and directions in Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese. Free of charge.

Climate and Weather Considerations:
Jeju features a humid subtropical climate strongly influenced by maritime weather patterns and Mount Hallasan's topographic rain shadow effect.
- Four Seasons:
  - Spring (March - May): Moderate temperatures (12°C - 18°C), flowering canola and cherry blossoms. High sea mist and variable coastal breezes.
  - Summer (June - August): Hot and humid (25°C - 32°C). Monsoon rainy season (Jangma) occurs typically from late June to mid-July.
  - Autumn (September - November): Crisp skies and stable hiking conditions (15°C - 22°C). Silver grass (Eulalia) coats the oreum volcanic cones.
  - Winter (December - February): Temperatures range from 3°C to 10°C, but strong Siberian winds (Noru wind) make the wind chill feel significantly colder. Heavy snow falls across Hallasan's highland ridges.
- Typhoon Preparedness:
  - Typhoon season spans July through September. During a Typhoon Advisory or Warning, maritime passenger ferries to Udo, Gapado, and Marado are suspended, flight schedules at Jeju Airport are delayed or canceled, and access to all Hallasan hiking trails is prohibited.

Emergency Services and Medical Facilities:
- Emergency Dispatch Numbers:
  - Police: 112
  - Fire and Ambulance (Medical Emergencies): 119
  - Maritime Distress (Coast Guard): 122
- Major Hospitals with International Patient Services:
  - Jeju National University Hospital (Aran 13-gil, Jeju City, Tel: 064-717-1114) - Tertiary university hospital with 24/7 emergency trauma care and English coordination.
  - Cheju Halla General Hospital (Doryeong-ro, Jeju City, Tel: 064-740-5000) - Level-1 emergency medical center and cardiovascular care center.
  - Seogwipo Medical Center (Jangsu-ro, Seogwipo City, Tel: 064-730-3000) - Primary public hospital serving southern Jeju Island.

Rental Car, EV Charging, and Driving Regulations:
- International Driving Permit (IDP): Foreign travelers must present a valid Geneva or Vienna Convention 1949/1968 IDP and original national driver's license.
- Electric Vehicle (EV) Infrastructure: Jeju is a leading eco-island with over 5,000 public EV charging stations installed at tourist sites, parking lots, and community offices.
- Speed Limits: The Island implements a strict Safe Speed 5030 policy (50 km/h on urban roads, 30 km/h in school zones). Automated speed cameras and average-speed measurement zones are widely deployed across Hallasan mountain passes.
