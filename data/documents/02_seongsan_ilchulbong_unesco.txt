Title: Seongsan Ilchulbong (Sunrise Peak) UNESCO World Heritage Guide
Source: Jeju Special Self-Governing Province & VisitJeju
Access Date: 2026-10-15
Category: UNESCO Natural Heritage & Volcanic Landmarks

Overview and Geological Significance:
Seongsan Ilchulbong, commonly called Sunrise Peak, is a 182-meter-high hydrovolcanic tuff cone situated on the eastern tip of Jeju Island. Formed roughly 5,000 to 10,000 years ago by Surtseyan hydromagmatic eruptions within shallow coastal waters, it features a gigantic bowl-shaped crater spanning 600 meters in diameter and 90 meters in depth. The outer ridge of the crater is crowned with 99 sharp basalt pinnacles, evoking the appearance of an ancient stone fortress rising from the emerald sea. Seongsan Ilchulbong was inscribed onto the UNESCO World Natural Heritage List in 2007 under the property "Jeju Volcanic Island and Lava Tubes."

Visiting Hours, Ticketing, and Admission:
- Operating Hours:
  - Summer Season (March to September): 07:00 to 20:00 (Ticket booth closes at 19:00).
  - Winter Season (October to February): 07:30 to 19:00 (Ticket booth closes at 17:50).
  - Closed: First Monday of every month for ecological preservation and maintenance.
- Admission Fees:
  - Adults (ages 25-64): 5,000 KRW.
  - Youth (ages 13-24) and Children (ages 7-12): 2,500 KRW.
  - Seniors (ages 65+) and Children under 6: Free admission with valid ID.
- Free Walking Course:
  - The northwestern coastal trail and lookout deck facing Udo Island are open free of charge without a summit ticket.

Summit Trail Information:
- Trail Length: Approximately 1.0 km round-trip.
- Duration: 20 to 30 minutes of uphill walking on well-maintained wooden staircases and volcanic stone decks; 20 minutes for descent.
- Trail Profile: Well-ventilated rest benches and panoramic viewpoints are installed at intervals.
- Recommended Viewing: Sunrise viewing is particularly celebrated on New Year's Day during the annual Seongsan Sunrise Festival.

Haenyeo Performance and Coastal Activities:
- Below the northern cliff of the peak, the Seongsan Haenyeo Performance takes place twice daily at 13:30 and 15:00 at the rocky beach (weather permitting).
- Visitors can observe Jeju's iconic female divers conducting underwater breath-hold dives and purchase fresh abalone, sea cucumber, and turban shells directly from the divers' cooperative.
- Speedboat tours operate from the adjacent rocky cove, providing ocean-side perspectives of the dramatic marine cliffs and crater erosion.
